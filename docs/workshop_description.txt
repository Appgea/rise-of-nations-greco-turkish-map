[h1]Greco-Turkish War: a real map for Rise of Nations: Extended Edition[/h1]
A 2 to 6 player map of Greece, the Aegean, western and central Anatolia, Cyprus and the Balkans up to the Danube, built from real geodata: coastlines, elevation, rivers and satellite imagery.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]512x512 real map (about 2.6 km per tile) with the Bosphorus, the Dardanelles and the Gulf of Corinth open
[*]Every city takes the name of the nearest real place: 291 places, with Turkish names for a Turks player and Greek-side names for every other nation. Captured cities are renamed by the new owner.
[*]Mountains on the real ranges, organic forests, 9 major rivers and 516 resources at real sites (Penteli marble, Ploiesti oil, Bor copper and more)
[*]Every start and every big historic city has a wood, a mineable rock and room for farms
[*]Set any slot to Computer (Rush or Boom, any difficulty) and change nations and teams on the setup screen
[*]Save and load at any time; the map script carries on after a load
[/list]

[h2]Scenarios[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]GrecoTurkish_Frontier[/b]: 2 players, Thessaloniki vs Bursa
[*][b]GrecoTurkish_Capitals[/b]: 2 players, Athens vs Ankara
[*][b]GrecoTurkish_4P[/b]: 4 players, Greeks vs Turks
[*][b]GrecoTurkish_6P[/b]: 6 players, adds Sofia and Konya
[/list]

[h2]How to play[/h2]
[b]Against bots:[/b] Solo Game > Quick Battle > Game Rules: Scenario > pick a GrecoTurkish scenario > set the other slots to Computer > Start.
[b]Online with friends:[/b] Multiplayer Game > Game Browser > Create > Game Rules: Scenario > pick a scenario > Invite Friend. Fill any remaining slots with Computer.
[b]Saves:[/b] Esc > Save Game / Load Game. Autosaves are kept too.
